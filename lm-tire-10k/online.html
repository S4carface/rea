The penny test

Is your tread too thin?

Put a penny in the tread, head down. If you can see the top of Lincoln's head, the tread is at 2/32 of an inch or less. That is the Massachusetts inspection minimum. Drag the slider to see what each depth means.

How old are your tires?

Find "DOT" on the side of the tire. The last 4 numbers are the week and year it was made. 2419 means week 24 of 2019.

Type the 4 numbers

Tire care in 30 seconds

Five habits that save you money.

  1. 01

    Check the pressure once a month

    Use the number on the sticker inside the driver's door, not the one on the tire. Check when the tires are cold.

    Cold-tire calculator →
  2. 02

    Rotate the tires

    Moving the tires around helps them wear evenly, so the set lasts longer. Your owner's manual says how often.

    Book a rotation →
  3. 03

    Watch the tread

    Thin tread slips on wet and snowy roads. The penny test takes ten seconds.

    Try the penny test →
  4. 04

    Don't ignore a shake

    A steering wheel that shakes at speed often means a wheel needs balancing.

    Book a balance →
  5. 05

    Fix small flats early

    A slow leak from a nail is often easier to deal with before the tire is driven flat.

Questions

Good to know.

Do you sell used tires?

Yes. We sell new and used tires. Call or text and ask what we have in your size.

Do you have winter tires?

Yes. We sell winter tires, and we install and balance them for you.

Can you fix my flat?

Yes. We do both plugs and patches. A plug works well for a small hole in the tread and is much quicker; a patch is also available. Damage in the sidewall means a new tire. Bring the car in and we'll check the tire.

What do you fix besides tires?

Wheel alignment, brakes, oil changes, suspension and steering, and check engine lights.

Do you do body work or paint?

No. We don't do body work or paint.

How much will it cost?

It depends on your car and your tire size. Call or text us and ask for a quote.

Do you speak Portuguese or Spanish?

Sim. Sí. For Portuguese, call or text 774-288-9675. For Spanish, call or text 304-806-6047.
