    Privacy policy

    Draft for review. Not published yet.

    What this website collects

    • This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies.
    • If you fill in a form, the details you type (like your name, phone number, car and the service you need) are used only to answer your request.
    • When you send a request by text, it goes from your phone to ours like any text message. The website does not keep a copy.

    Saved details on your device

    • The appointment form can remember your name, phone and car on this device, only if you turn that option on. It is off unless you choose it.
    • You can clear it any time with the "Forget my details" button, or by clearing your browser data.
    • Display settings (like larger text) are saved on your device so the site looks the same next time.

    Visit statistics

    • If we turn on visit statistics, we will use a privacy-friendly service that does not use cookies and does not identify you. It counts things like page views and button taps.

    Services from other companies

    • Buttons for Google Maps, Google reviews, WhatsApp and Acima open those companies' websites or apps, and their privacy policies apply there.
    • Vehicle lists in our forms may come from the U.S. government vehicle database (NHTSA). Only the year and make you pick are sent to it.

    Your choices

    • You can ask us what information we have from your requests, or ask us to delete it. Call 508-853-3322.
