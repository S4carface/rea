    Dashboard warning lights

    Red usually means stop soon and safely. Yellow or orange means have it checked soon. A flashing light is more urgent than a steady one.

    • Check engine light

      Steady: an engine or emissions problem was detected. Have the codes read soon. Flashing: an engine misfire that can damage the catalytic converter. Slow down and get it checked right away.

    • Tire pressure light (TPMS)

      One or more tires is low. Check all four with a gauge and fill to the number on the door sticker. If it flashes and then stays on, the sensor system needs service.

    • Brake warning light

      The parking brake may be on, or the brake fluid is low or there is a brake system problem. If it stays on with the parking brake off, have it checked before driving far.

    • Oil pressure light

      Low oil pressure can ruin the engine. Pull over safely, turn the engine off and check the oil level. Don't keep driving with this light on.

    • Battery / charging light

      The charging system may not be charging the battery. The car can run out of power. Get it checked soon.

    • Engine temperature light

      The engine is overheating. Pull over safely and turn it off. Don't open the radiator cap while it is hot.

    Maintenance reminder or warning code?

    "Oil change due", "Service" or an oil life percentage is a maintenance reminder. You reset it after the service is done. The steps are different for every car: your owner's manual shows how for your model.

    A warning light like check engine means the car stored a trouble code. Clearing the code with a scanner turns the light off but does not fix anything, and it erases information that helps find the problem.

    We read the codes, explain what they mean and give you the price before any repair.
