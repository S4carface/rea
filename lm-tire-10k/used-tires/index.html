Used tires in Worcester

Used tires cost less up front. Text us your tire size and we will tell you what we have in stock and the price. We install and balance them on your car.

What we do

  • Used tires in many common sizes
  • Installation and balancing
  • Replace one tire, a pair, or all four
  • New tires too, if we don't have your size used

Come in if you notice

  • A damaged tire you need to replace soon
  • One worn tire that no longer matches the others
  • You want to save money up front
