## What happens if a CHECK item can't be confirmed before launch
- Music: the new site launches without background music (it was already off by default). A replacement with a clear commercial license can be added later, after L&M Tire approves any cost.
- Grind and clunk: replace with free commercial-license (CC0) recordings of similar quality, or new recordings made at the shop. No money is spent without L&M Tire's approval.
