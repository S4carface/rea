    Shop policies

    Plain answers about how we work. Tap a topic to open it. Questions? Call 508-853-3322.

    Appointments and deposit
    • A $60 deposit is required for designated mechanical service appointments.
    • The deposit is credited toward your service.
    • Routine walk-in tire services generally do not require a deposit.
    • You may reschedule once with at least 24 hours' notice.
    • Late cancellations and no-shows may result in losing the deposit. We tell you this when you book.
    • Special-order tires must be paid in full before we order them.
    • Appointment requests are not confirmed until our staff confirms availability.
    Payment
    • We accept cash and credit cards.
    • Full payment is required before vehicle pickup.
    Tires
    • Tire disposal is $10 per tire.
    • Special-order tires must be paid in full before we order them.
    • Installed tires are generally final sale, subject to applicable law.
    • AWD and 4WD vehicles may need matching tires to protect the drivetrain.
    • After tire or wheel service, please follow our wheel lug nut retorque instructions.
    Repairs and diagnostics
    • Diagnostic services are charged separately from repairs.
    • We will not do additional repairs without your authorization.
    Customer-supplied parts and tires
    • We accept customer-supplied parts and tires at our discretion.
    • We do not provide a product warranty on customer-supplied parts.
    Returns
    • Eligible product returns may be subject to a 20% restocking fee.
    • Installed tires are generally final sale, subject to applicable law.
    Vehicle pickup and storage
    • Full payment is required before vehicle pickup.
    • Vehicle storage is $35 per day after 24 hours following documented pickup notification, subject to applicable law.
    Acima
    • We offer Acima lease-to-own options for eligible parts only. Labor is not included. Ask us which parts qualify.
    • Approval is not guaranteed
    • Acima's terms apply

    Nothing on this page limits your rights under Massachusetts or federal consumer protection law.
