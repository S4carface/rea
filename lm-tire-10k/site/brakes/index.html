    Brake repair in Worcester

    Squealing, grinding or a soft pedal are signs your brakes need a look. Bring the car in. We check the brakes and tell you what they need and the price before we start.

    Come in if you notice

    • Squealing or grinding when you stop
    • The car shakes or pulls when you brake
    • The pedal feels soft or goes low
    • The brake warning light is on

    Every car is different, so we give you the price before any work starts.

    Questions

    Is a squeal dangerous?

    A squeal often means the brakes need attention soon. Grinding is more urgent. It is safer to have them checked.

    How much does a brake job cost?

    It depends on your car and what it needs. We check first and give you the price before any work starts.
