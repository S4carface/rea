    What is that noise?

    Tap a sound to hear it. These are examples: the same noise can have different causes, so bring the car in to find out.

    High squeal when braking

    Often the brake pad wear indicator, a small metal tab that squeals when the pads get thin. Can also be dust or glazed pads.

    Brakes

    Grinding when braking

    Often the pads are worn through and metal is touching the rotor. This can damage the rotors fast. Get it checked soon.

    Brakes

    Clunk over bumps

    Often a worn suspension or steering part, like a sway bar link, ball joint or strut mount.
