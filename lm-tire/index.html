Tire tips

Keep your tires safe

Tread

Do the penny test

Put a penny in the tread with Lincoln's head upside down. If you can see all of his head, the tread is too low and it's time for new tires.

Air

Check the air once a month

Check when the tires are cold. The right pressure for your car is on a sticker inside the driver's door.

Winter

Cold weather lowers tire pressure

Tires lose about 1 PSI for every 10°F drop. When the tire light comes on after a cold night, add air.

Wear

Rotate your tires

Rotating about every 5,000 to 7,500 miles helps them wear evenly and last longer.
